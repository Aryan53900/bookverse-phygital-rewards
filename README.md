I want to build BookVerse – a phygital social platform for book lovers that solves the problem of fragmented book communities and lack of tangible rewards for reader engagement. Today, while readers actively share reviews and participate in discussions online, there is no system that connects their passion for books to real-world benefits. On the other hand, Web3 rewards exist but are often complex and not tied to everyday hobbies like reading.

BookVerse bridges this gap by combining a social platform for reviews and communities, gamification mechanics (streaks, achievements, leaderboards), and Web3 NFT rewards that can be redeemed at partner bookstores. This makes reading more engaging, socially rewarding, and financially beneficial while also driving real-world bookstore traffic and providing a simple Web3 use case for mass adoption.
